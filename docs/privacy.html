Privacy Policy for WhoStarts

Last updated: May 20, 2026

WhoStarts does not collect, store, share, or sell personal data. The app is designed as a local party game helper and does not require an account.

Data stored on your device

WhoStarts may store game settings, score history, dice and coin history, and premium access status locally on your device. This data is not sent to us or to our servers.

Purchases

WhoStarts uses Google Play Billing to process optional premium purchases. Payment processing is handled by Google Play. We do not receive or store payment card details.

Location, advertising, and analytics

  • WhoStarts does not collect location data.
  • WhoStarts does not use advertising SDKs.
  • WhoStarts does not use analytics SDKs.
  • WhoStarts does not use account login or user profiles.

Deleting local data

You can delete locally stored app data by uninstalling the app or clearing the app data in your device settings.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact us at sirmaxen.business@gmail.com.
